# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord application (the "Application") operated by the application owner ("Operator") may process information when the Application is authorized or used in a Discord server.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies only to information processed by the Application. Discord independently processes information under its own privacy policy and platform terms.

## 2. Information the Application may process

The Application may process limited Discord data necessary to provide server-administration functionality, including:

- Discord user IDs;
- Discord server (guild) IDs;
- channel and category IDs and names;
- role IDs, role names, and permission data;
- server configuration and permission-overwrite data;
- message IDs and message content when a requested feature requires reading, creating, editing, or managing messages;
- webhook, integration, or application configuration metadata where required for an authorized administrative task;
- command inputs, requested administrative actions, and technical error information; and
- other Discord API data that is directly necessary to perform a feature requested by an authorized user.

The Application is not intended to collect information unrelated to its administrative functionality.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

- authenticate and authorize requested actions;
- create, inspect, configure, or manage Discord server resources;
- execute administrative requests submitted by authorized users or software agents;
- maintain security and prevent unauthorized use;
- diagnose errors and maintain the Application; and
- comply with applicable legal or platform requirements.

The Operator does not sell personal information and does not use Discord API data for advertising or unrelated profiling.

## 4. Message content

The Application may access message content only where the requested functionality requires it and where Discord permissions and applicable privileged-intent requirements permit such access.

Message content should not be retained longer than necessary for the relevant function unless retention is required for a clearly defined operational, security, or legal purpose.

## 5. Data retention

The Application should retain Discord data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality, maintain security, diagnose technical issues, or satisfy legal obligations.

Temporary data may be discarded after a task completes. Configuration data or operational logs may be retained where necessary for continued operation, troubleshooting, or abuse prevention.

If the Application's implementation changes to introduce additional persistent storage, this Privacy Policy should be updated to describe that storage accurately.

## 6. Data sharing

Information may be transmitted to service providers strictly as necessary to operate the Application, such as hosting infrastructure, software libraries, automation systems, or other services used to execute authorized Discord administration tasks.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of users, Discord servers, the Application, or the Operator.

The Operator does not sell Discord user data to third parties.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect credentials and data handled by the Application. Discord bot tokens, API credentials, and other secrets should not be publicly disclosed or embedded in public repositories.

No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. User and server-owner choices

Discord server owners and administrators control whether the Application is authorized in their server and which permissions are granted to it. They may remove the Application from a server or revoke its permissions through Discord.

Where applicable, requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of data controlled by the Operator may be submitted using the contact method below.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Application is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children independently of Discord's platform. The Application should only be used in accordance with Discord's age requirements and applicable law.

## 10. Third-party services

Discord and any third-party services used by the Application operate under their own privacy policies. This policy does not control how those independent services process information.

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the Application's features, storage practices, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.

## 12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be directed to the Operator through the GitHub account or repository hosting this document, or through the Discord server in which the Application is operated.
